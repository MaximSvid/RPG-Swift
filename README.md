# RPG Swift

## 🏆 Hero Battle – Turn-Based Strategy Game

### Hero Battle is a small turn-based game written in Swift. The game features two teams: heroes and enemies.

### 🎮 Gameplay
	•	Hero Team: Players can manually choose different attack types. There are three heroes, each with three unique attacks.
	•	Enemy Team: Enemies attack randomly and have a chance to summon a powerful boss for assistance.
	•	Strategic Decisions: Choose the best attack combinations to defeat your enemies.
	•	Dynamic Battles: Every match is different due to random enemy actions and boss summons.

### 🔥 Features
✅ Turn-based combat – Make strategic choices in every turn.
✅ Unique hero abilities – Each hero has a unique playstyle and skills.
✅ Randomized enemy attacks – No battle plays out the same way twice.
✅ Boss fights – Enemies can call for reinforcements when things get tough.
✅ Simple & fun gameplay – Easy to play, but with room for strategy.
